# Code of conduct

EA Explorer follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. Its full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>; it applies in every place the project meets:
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel in its name.

In short, in our own words:

- **Our pledge**: we make taking part a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability,
  ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual
  identity and orientation.
- **What we expect**: kindness and respect; patience with people who are learning; giving and taking feedback
  gracefully; owning our mistakes and learning from them; focusing on what is best for the community.
- **What we do not accept**: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks,
  harassment in public or private, publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that
  would be inappropriate in a professional setting.
- **Enforcement**: the maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions,
  or ban, temporarily or for good, anyone who breaks them, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (a
  correction, a warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban).
- **Reporting**: tell the maintainer privately (the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Every report is reviewed
  promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.
