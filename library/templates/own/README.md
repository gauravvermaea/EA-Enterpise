# Your own template libraries

Your organisation's templates are uploaded as a **template library** (D264), not loaded from this folder:

1. **Administration > Project library** (or the *Template libraries* step of the project wizard): **⬇ zip** on
   EA Explorer's library. It has a folder for every deliverable and artifact
   (`09_ADM_Phase_Workspaces/<phase>/Deliverables or Artifacts/...`) and a starter file in each.
2. Rebrand or replace the files, keeping them in their folders, and add more documents to any folder.
3. Zip the folders again and upload the zip as a new library, under a name of its own (e.g. *Thotnr 2027*).

A library you add in the wizard is yours and your project's until a Super Admin offers it to every project. You may keep
your working copy of such a zip here (this folder is committed to git: put here only what you may share).
