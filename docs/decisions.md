| D130 | Metamodel draft review in rounds: a reviewer (Model Creators and Library Admins for a new root, the target model's Model Admins for changes, Super Admins) applies a draft, **sends it back for changes** with comments on the whole draft or on single items, or rejects it. A sent-back draft is editable by its maker (or their AI through MCP, which reads the comments) and is resubmitted as a new round; a rejected draft can be reopened as a new round. Comments are marked fixed; every step is kept with the metamodel as it stood, and the reviewer sees what changed since the last review. Detailed editing is meant to happen after applying, in the new model's metamodel editor, where the model's purpose and source are known |
| D131 | Every model a person creates (project, root, library, upload, promotion, framework move) starts as a **draft** and is not published straight away: its Model Admins and the people they give roles work on it, and nobody else may create a model from it. | D132 | Copies, uploads, downloads, promotions and framework moves choose how much data to take: **without data** (the metamodel only), **with the data**, or **deep** (the data with its full history). A deep package (Excel or zip) carries the content history (readable Change history sheet, and History data loaded again on upload) and the metamodel's versions and changes (readable) |
| D133 | Every copied row keeps the guid of the row it was copied from (origin_guid): entities, attribute values, relationships and every metamodel item, in copies and in uploads (a package's keys are the source guids).
