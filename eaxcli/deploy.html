Deploy EA Explorer

eax deploy: the same steps as in the console. Nothing is changed until you choose Deploy.

Where and how

Deploying

    Check your installation. In EA Explorer: System admin › Self-test › Smoke test › Start (it prepares what it needs). Or on the command line: eax test --smoke; from another machine: eax testbench --target https://<address> --i-own-this --smoke. Results: Self-test › History and the report (docs/deployment.md, "Check your installation").
